Questions

What people ask before they buy.

My Mac has no notch. Does it work?

Yes. With a notch, the island extends it. Without one, it only appears when it has something to show, or when you rest the cursor for a moment at the top center of the screen; a thin sliver signals the approach. With several displays, the resting island follows the cursor.

Which Mac do I need?

An Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) running macOS 26. Fronton doesn’t exist for Intel Macs: it won’t launch on one, even under macOS 26.

There are free notch apps. Why pay?

Fronton lives on its price alone: no ads, no telemetry, no account to create. The 14-day trial opens everything, with no payment details: you judge it on your own Mac before you pay.

Lifetime or monthly: what’s the difference?

Not one feature. Lifetime: one payment, and updates for as long as Fronton exists. Monthly: cancel anytime, the key goes dark at the end of the paid period, and Fronton has to check in at least once every 14 days.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your settings stay. Apple Music, Spotify and a timer already running keep showing in the island; the panel still opens, blurred and locked, until you enter a key. During the trial, the panel shows how much time is left.

How do I activate my key, and on how many Macs?

The key arrives by email after the purchase: paste it into Settings › Account. It covers two Macs. To move to another Mac, click “Release this Mac” on the old one; if it is no longer yours, remove it from the Polar customer portal, then activate the new one right away.

Spotify or Apple Music?

Both: title, artwork, controls, and lyrics once you turn them on. If both are playing, Fronton shows whichever you started last.

Where does my data go?

The app has no account, no Fronton server, no usage statistics. The AI runs on the Mac, and clipboard history never leaves your Mac (and is only kept for the time you choose). What does leave — the license check, the weather — is listed on the Legal page.

Will the local AI slow my Mac down?

Not at rest: the model only loads when an AI gesture is coming, and gives the memory back at most two minutes after the last use. Its 3.4 GB download only happens if you ask for it.
